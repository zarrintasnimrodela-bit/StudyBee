About StudyBee

A cleaner way to find BRACU course resources.

StudyBee is a student-made BRACU course resource hub that organizes publicly shared materials, links, videos, notes, questions, and useful files in one place.

It is built to help students find resources faster without scrolling through scattered posts, folders, and links.

Course materials may vary by semester, so always follow your current faculty instructions.
