About StudyBee

A cleaner way to find BRACU course resources.

StudyBee is a student-made resource hub that organizes publicly shared materials, links, videos, notes, questions, and useful files in one place.

Favorites and recently viewed courses are saved only in your current browser.

Course materials may vary by semester, so always follow your current faculty instructions.
