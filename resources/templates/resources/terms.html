Terms of use

Use StudyBee responsibly

StudyBee organizes student-shared and publicly available study materials. It is not an official BRAC University service.

Resources

Materials may be incomplete, outdated, or specific to a past semester. Students remain responsible for following current faculty instructions.

Contributions

Submit only materials you are allowed to share. Do not upload private student information, leaked examinations, copyrighted books without permission, or harmful files.

Moderation

Administrators may reject, hide, edit, or remove resources and may restrict abusive use of the submission or reporting systems.

Availability

StudyBee may change or become temporarily unavailable. No guarantee is made that every file or external link will remain accessible.
