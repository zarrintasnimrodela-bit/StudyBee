About StudyBee

A cleaner way to find BRACU course resources.

StudyBee is a student-made resource hub that organizes publicly shared materials, links, videos, notes, questions, and useful files in one place.

Favorites and recently viewed courses are saved only in your current browser.

Course materials may vary by semester, so always follow your current faculty instructions.

Privacy

How StudyBee uses your information

StudyBee is a student-made resource hub. Public browsing does not require an account.

Student login and sign up

Sign up requires a one-time code sent to a BRACU email. Password reset also uses a one-time code. Normal logins use the separate StudyBee password you create. StudyBee never asks for your BRACU Google password, and the StudyBee password is stored only as a secure hash.

Submissions and reports

Submission and report details are stored so administrators can review them. Your email may be used to send receipt, approval, rejection, or resolution updates.

Browser storage

Favorites and recently viewed courses are stored locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to StudyBee.

Files and hosting

Uploaded resources may be stored in StudyBee’s configured cloud storage. Do not submit private, restricted, or personally sensitive documents.

Terms of use

Use StudyBee responsibly

StudyBee organizes student-shared and publicly available study materials. It is not an official BRAC University service.

Resources

Materials may be incomplete, outdated, or specific to a past semester. Students remain responsible for following current faculty instructions.

Contributions

Submit only materials you are allowed to share. Do not upload private student information, leaked examinations, copyrighted books without permission, or harmful files.

Moderation

Administrators may reject, hide, edit, or remove resources and may restrict abusive use of the submission or reporting systems.

Availability

StudyBee may change or become temporarily unavailable. No guarantee is made that every file or external link will remain accessible.
