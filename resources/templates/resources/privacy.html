Privacy

How StudyBee uses your information

StudyBee is a student-made resource hub. Public browsing does not require an account.

Student login and sign up

New accounts must verify a BRACU email using a one-time code, then create a separate StudyBee password. Normal logins use that StudyBee password. Password-reset requests also require an emailed code. StudyBee never asks for your BRACU Google password, and StudyBee passwords are stored only as secure hashes.

Submissions and reports

Submission and report details are stored so administrators can review them. Your email may be used to send receipt, approval, rejection, or resolution updates.

Browser storage

Favorites and recently viewed courses are stored locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to StudyBee.

Files and hosting

Uploaded resources may be stored in StudyBee’s configured cloud storage. Do not submit private, restricted, or personally sensitive documents.
