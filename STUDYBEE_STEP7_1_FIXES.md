# StudyBee Step 7.1 — Publication and Moderation Fixes

- Approved student submissions are published as community resources, not officially verified resources.
- Existing Approved submissions without a published Resource are repaired automatically by migration 0016.
- Submission status is read-only in Admin; use “Approve and publish selected submissions”.
- Public pages show only Unverified and Verified records internally, but no verification badge or date is displayed.
- Reporting any linked resource immediately marks it Needs review and removes it from public course pages and global search.
- Needs review and Broken resources remain available in Admin.
- Bulk imports create normal resources and appear under their matching course and in global search.
- The sample import creates “Sample Lecture Notes” under CSE260.
